# Editorial record: the law-firm billing article

Published: 28 September 2026 at `/guides/law-firm-billing-leakage`.
Source draft: a pitch Phila pasted in from another AI chat. The voice and
structure were kept; each figure and promise was checked before it went on
the page. `scripts/verify-law-article.mjs copy` fails if any rejected claim
comes back.

## Claim by claim

| Pitch said | Verdict | What the page says instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Firms collect only 89–90% of what they bill | **Wrong.** Clio's 2025 Legal Trends benchmarks put the average collection rate at 93% | 93% collected; on R50M invoiced, R3.5M never arrives |
| "47 cents of every rand evaporates" | **Not a measurement.** It comes from one blog's worked example (65% × 90% × 90%) | Clio's measured chain: 3.0 hours recorded, 2.6 invoiced, 2.4 collected of an 8-hour day; about R82 banked per R100 recorded (88% × 93%) |
| Admin overhead is 25–35% of total costs | **No source found** | Dropped |
| 54% of lawyers report exhaustion | **Misread.** The Rev survey's 54% is AI users who felt *less* stressed | 8am's 2026 Admin Misery Index (400 legal professionals, via Above the Law): 39% lose 4+ hours a week to admin, 63% work outside normal hours, 22% missed a deadline because of admin |
| Lawyers take on 11% more admin every year | **No source found** | Dropped |
| 75 days from work to payment | **Confirmed.** Clio 2025 median lockup: 43 days to bill + 32 days to collect | Kept |
| Junior associate R600k, practice manager R500k, admin R300k | **No source** | Turned into a question: "Write the total down" |
| "Every single law firm I walk into…" | **Not true.** GrowthCred has no published law-firm client | "Every owner I sit down with…"; and the page says plainly there's no law-firm case study yet |
| "I've never had someone… say no thanks" | **Invented proof** | Dropped |
| "If it doesn't work, you don't pay. You keep what I built." | **Extends the published guarantee** | The homepage guarantee, word for word: "If the Command Core doesn't give you back at least 20% of your week, the engagement is on us." |
| "Costs less than one hire and does the work of three" | **No published price to support it** | "You get a quote after we've scoped your firm… If hiring wins, hire." |
| Billing cycle under 30 days; proposals same day; intake in minutes | **Outcome promises** | Framed as the targets we build and measure against |
| One day for first systems, 90 days full build, results in two weeks | **Matches the published offer** (Velocity, Integration, "first result in 14 days") | Kept |

## Boundaries kept

The article states that the system does no legal research, gives no advice,
files nothing and never touches the trust account, and that a practitioner
checks every authority. The Legal Practice Council's 2026 ethics guide is cited
for why.
