## 8.1 Main workshop VSL (long-form)

**[Hook]** If you are the kind of business owner who works nights and weekends and still feels behind, this is going to be the most useful few minutes you spend today. Because in the next little while I am going to show you how to get 10 hours of your week back, in a single day, without hiring anyone and without becoming a tech person.

**[The problem, the despair]** Here is the trap you are in. Your business runs on you. Every important thing goes through your head, your inbox, your hands. That felt fine when you started. Now it is the ceiling. You cannot grow, because growing means more of the exact work that is already drowning you. You cannot step away, because the moment you do, everything slows down. You are not running a business. You are the business, and it is running you.

And you have tried AI. You have opened the chat, typed a question, got something generic, and thought "this does not know my business". So you closed it. Because every time you use it, you have to explain everything from scratch, and by then you may as well have done it yourself.

**[The turn, the desire]** Now imagine the opposite. You sit down in the morning and the work is already moving. The research is done. The follow-ups are drafted, in your voice. The busy work that used to eat your day is handled by systems that know your business as well as you do, because you built them to. You are not doing the work. You are deciding what matters and letting the systems do the rest. That is the difference between an operator and an owner. And it is closer than you think.

**[The mechanism]** The reason most people never get there is that they treat AI like a tool you pick up and put down. The operators who get their time back do one thing differently. They give their business a brain: one place that holds everything about how they work, and they wire an AI worker to it, so every task starts already knowing the business. No re-explaining. No blank page. That is the whole shift, and it is not complicated. It is just not obvious until someone shows you.

**[The offer]** That is what we do in one day, together, in a room. You do not watch. You build. By the end of the day your brain is built, your worker is wired to it, your first real tasks are handed over, and there is a routine running overnight so the work keeps moving while you sleep. You walk in an operator. You walk out an owner, with the systems already live.

**[The guarantee]** And here is how sure I am. Get the 10 hours back, or you don't pay. Come, do the builds, use the systems for 30 days. If you have not got the time back, email us and we refund you in full. The risk is ours, not yours.

**[The close]** The seats are limited, because we keep the room small enough to get everyone across the line on the day. If you are tired of being the bottleneck in your own business, book your seat now, and let us hand you your week back.

## 8.2 Free class

**[Hook]** In the next few minutes I am going to show you exactly where your week is disappearing, and the one shift that gets 10 hours of it back. No cost, no catch. Let us get into it.

**[Teach, beat 1: find the leak]** First, the leak. Most owners think they are busy with big important work. When you actually map it, most of the week is the same handful of repeated tasks, the questions you answer twice, the work that only you can do because it all lives in your head. Walk through your own week here. Point at the ten hours. _[Teach the mapping exercise.]_

**[Teach, beat 2: why AI has not helped yet]** Second, why AI has not fixed this for you yet. You have been using it like a search box. You ask, it forgets, you re-explain. The operators getting real time back do the opposite: they build the business a brain, so the AI never forgets who they are. _[Explain the brain concept simply.]_

**[Teach, beat 3: the first move]** Third, the move you can make today. _[Teach one small, real first step they can take for free, so they feel a win and trust the method.]_

**[Close, pitch the workshop]** Now, you can do this piece by piece on your own, and some of you will. But if you want it all built and running in a single day, with us in the room making sure you leave with it live, that is exactly what the workshop is for. Same shift, done with you, in a day, guaranteed. If that is what you want, the next step is on the screen. Either way, go plug that first leak today.
